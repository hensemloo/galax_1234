What the AI can and can't do

  • It only sees the outside of the pod in the photo.
  • It only recognises the conditions listed above. Anything else may be labelled wrongly.
  • Blurry, dark or cluttered photos reduce accuracy. Exclude unclear photos.
  • The officer's final label is what counts, not the AI's suggestion.
  • This is preliminary screening, not an official diagnosis.
